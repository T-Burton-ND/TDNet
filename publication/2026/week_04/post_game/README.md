# TDNet 2026 Week 4 postgame scoring reconstruction

Week 4 was not included in the original postgame scoring directory. For the Week 5 cumulative package, it has now been scored from the immutable Week 4 pregame bundle and the cached completed results. No model was rerun and no frozen prediction was changed.

The reconstructed source matched all 58 frozen game IDs. It has zero duplicate IDs, missing IDs, or missing final scores. The machine-readable certification and source hashes are in `metadata/results_completeness.json`.

This directory now includes operational predictions, the 42-model F0–F6 scientific cohort, and the separate full F0–F8 scientific cohort. The season-to-date cumulative figures through Week 5 are published in `publication/2026/week_05/post_game`.
