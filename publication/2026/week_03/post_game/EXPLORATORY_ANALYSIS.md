# Exploratory Week 3 diagnostics

These analyses were selected after outcomes were available and are descriptive, not confirmatory.

- Calibration: the 50–60% bucket was closest to observed winner accuracy (gap -10.3%, n=20).
- Market context: the ≤3.5-point closing-spread bucket had the largest TDNet margin MAE (12.10 points, n=12).
- Disagreement: consensus MAE was 10.17 points in the low-disagreement tertile and 10.86 in the high-disagreement tertile. Spearman rho was 0.12 (game bootstrap 95% interval -0.13 to 0.37).
