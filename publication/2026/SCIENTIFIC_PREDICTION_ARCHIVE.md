The scientific packages are distinct from TDNet's operational/public prediction
roster. They preserve full model-level predictions, full ballots, consensus power
rankings, paper figures, generation provenance, and the declared immutable state.
Market-bearing F7 and F8 tiers are excluded.

This archive is a permanent public-Git exception. Future weekly scientific
pre-game predictions and post-game results—including the scientific consensus
results PNG—are retained, hash-verified, committed, and pushed with the weekly
publication. Operational/private frozen bundles remain outside this exception.
