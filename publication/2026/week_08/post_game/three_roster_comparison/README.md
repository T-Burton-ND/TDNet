# Week 8 three-roster comparison

All three consensus scorecards use the same 49 games with F20 forecasts. The margin-wide and F0–F6 rosters also cover all 53 frozen Week 8 games in their primary scorecards. The expanded F0–F20 roster has 126 cells; F20 probabilities were issued as late pregame research forecasts, while F20 margins and ATS are postgame applications of pregame-frozen models to archived pregame expert inputs. ATS assumes -110 and the moneyline uses saved pregame quotes. These bankroll curves are descriptive.
