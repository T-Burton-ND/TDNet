# Scientific-roster metric heatmaps

The figure suite contains one heatmap each for MAE, upset recall, chalk recall,
winner accuracy, Brier score, and ATS accuracy. Every figure has separate
margin- and winner-objective panels, scientific-model rows (M1, M2, M3, M4,
M5, M10), and fingerprint columns. F6-C appears between F6 and the market
benchmarks F7/F8.

Only complete ten-fold hyperparameter configurations are eligible. MAE, winner
accuracy, and Brier score are weighted by held-out game count. ATS accuracy is
weighted by the number of graded non-push decisions. Chalk and upset recall are
macro-averaged across folds because the legacy result schema retained the rates
but not their class denominators; this limitation is stated in captions and the
underlying fold table is published beside the figures.

Lower-is-better metrics (MAE and Brier) use reversed color scales. Higher-is-
better metrics use forward scales. Cells are numerically annotated, missing
values remain visibly blank, and both panels of a metric share one color range.
PNG, SVG, and PDF outputs are generated by
`python -m gridiron_ml.cli.publication.build_scientific_metric_heatmaps`.
