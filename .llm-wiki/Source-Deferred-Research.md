# Deferred Research Source

The deferred-items registry, seasonal change policy, time-adjusted fingerprint note, rehearsal-data policy, and master-plan P2 list distinguish pending evidence from exploratory future work and temporary operating procedures.

## Contribution

These records protect the frozen 2026 evaluation from post-freeze selection while naming work that could be reconsidered after prospective outcomes or in a future-season namespace.

## Methods and directions

- `DEFERRED_ITEMS_REGISTRY.yaml` keeps prospective outcomes, input snapshot, provider timestamps, and final paper conclusions behind explicit evidence triggers. It identifies MoE as a 2027 candidate in a separate namespace.
- The seasonal change policy bars official 2026 retraining/recalibration on 2026 outcomes, outcome-driven roster or weights, new official features, and postgame selection; those belong in experimental or future-season work.
- `time_adjusted_fingerprints.md` defines t2.1 same-week z, t2.2 phase z, and t2.3 recency-week z transformations using prior seasons, but says not to submit the proposed source × time-method × top-k × objective search yet.
- Master-plan P2 candidates include structured neural and alternative tabular neural models, exploratory weekly refits, injury/roster features, new fingerprint families, post-freeze ensembles, expanded SHAP, and conference-specific models.
- The preseason rehearsal-data policy permits Coaches Poll as an AP surrogate and carries 2025 talent/returning production only for a non-public rehearsal if 2026 provider tables are absent. These values are not provider observations, frozen predictions, or release artifacts.
