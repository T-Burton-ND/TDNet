# Deferred and Abandoned Directions

This page separates explicitly superseded or dropped work from research deferred by the frozen 2026 design, unfinished operational evidence, and temporary rehearsal procedures.

## Deferred pending triggers or future season

- Prospective outcome collection and scoring remain pending until 2026 results exist; missing outcomes block claiming the prospective study is complete, but did not block the retrospective/code release.
- Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) is a 2027 candidate in a separate namespace. The frozen protocol excludes MoE, dynamic routing, learned stacking weights, transformers, graph neural networks, and winner-only operational models.
- Time-adjusted fingerprints t2.1 (same-week z), t2.2 (phase z), and t2.3 (recency-week z) are documented as candidate methods, but their proposed source × time-method × top-k × objective search explicitly says “Do not submit this yet.” They are not part of the canonical ladder.
- Master-plan P2 ideas include structured neural models, an alternative tabular neural architecture, exploratory weekly refits, injury/roster features, new fingerprint families, post-freeze ensembles, expanded SHAP, and conference-specific models. These are future/experimental directions, not approved modifications to the frozen system.
- The seasonal policy bars official 2026 retraining/recalibration with 2026 outcomes, outcome-driven roster or weight changes, new official features, and postgame selection. Such work belongs in EXPERIMENTAL or 2027 candidate work.

## Temporary rehearsal choices (not abandoned project directions)

For a non-public preseason rehearsal only, the Coaches Poll can stand in as an AP surrogate until the AP Top 25 exists; 2025 talent/returning production can be carried forward if 2026 tables are unavailable. These values must not be represented as provider observations, written into the raw cache, or treated as frozen predictions or release artifacts. This is a temporary fail-closed rehearsal policy, not a permanent replacement for official data.

## Incomplete snapshot versus later completion

The July 20 storage incident left many arrays incomplete and status files stale. Later dated release records report corrected F0–F8 refits, corrected HPS, and four completed 2025 regeneration packages. Thus the 33,540-task remaining count describes that July snapshot only, not present work.
