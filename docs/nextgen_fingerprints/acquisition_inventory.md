# Acquisition inventory and launch checks

This is a **plan**, not an acquisition run. On 2026-09-25, `data/raw/cfbd/v2` held about 32 MB of local cached Parquets. Core games, team-game stats, advanced game stats, havoc, PPA games, coaches, recruiting teams, talent, returning, lines, and pregame win probability had files named 2010–2026; roster and recruiting-player files existed for only ten years. File presence alone does **not** prove endpoint, row, or as-of completeness. The existing standalone fetcher is `src/gridiron_ml/pipeline/fetch/cfbd_fetch_v2.py`.

The [current CFBD reference](https://api.collegefootballdata.com/api/plays) requires year/week on `/plays` and caps `/plays/stats` at 2,000 rows. [Provider coverage](https://api.collegefootballdata.com/data-availability) places raw plays and drives from 2001, play-level player attribution from 2012, player usage from 2013, returning production from 2014, talent from 2015, portal from 2021, and enriched passing/rushing from 2025. Actual completeness varies by game and field. Empty responses inside these eras require investigation, not automatic zero imputation. The [player API](https://api.collegefootballdata.com/api/players) documents `/player/portal` and `/player/usage`.

The 2026-09-26 offline pre-Stage-A manifest, using legacy schedules only to estimate request identities, enumerated **245 year/week `/plays` partitions**, with one smoke-tested and **244 remaining**. It has **1,756 planned requests**, including **16 fresh `/games` calls**, and **1 ledger-backed reusable `/plays` partition**. Legacy `/games` is never reused. The earlier 1,500/257 plan treated 16 legacy schedules and 240 team-game partitions as ready before schedule authority was established. After Stage A completes, rerun preflight against the 16 fresh schedules; week/game request counts and `/games/teams` reuses may change. `/drives` has 16 year requests in that plan. The uncommitted full `/plays/stats` game-partition scenario adds **11,509 game requests** for 2012–2025 before retries or cap subdivisions. Combined with the base plan, that would be **13,265 first attempts** plus retries and quota checks, leaving **6,731 local attempt slots** after the four already reserved. These are provisional scenario counts, not approved requests or fetched results. The combined projected footprint is **89,771,158,608 bytes (89.77 GB)** against the 100 GiB soft limit. Stage E must sample first, verify stat meaning, coverage, and cap handling, identify concrete F10/F12 features uniquely enabled, then choose full, subset, or skip by incremental value, fresh-schedule calls, quota, and storage. See the machine-readable `results/preflight/cfbd_plan_summary_v1.json` and `configs/experiments/nextgen_plays_stats_audit_v1.json`.

The experiment enforces **20,000 actual outbound attempts maximum**, including retries and quota checks, and preserves at least **10,000** of the 30,000 monthly account calls. The 2026-09-25 live `/info` response reported 1 used and 29,999 remaining before the play smoke; the local reservation ledger recorded 4 outbound attempts afterward. The two counters have different timing and scope. Before each future stage, the local atomic ledger limits every attempt; the live quota check ensures the remaining local budget can be spent while preserving the provider reserve, without requiring all pending requests times three to fit at once. Check free storage too. The budget and request ledgers live under `/groups/bsavoie2/tburton2/TDNet/fingerprint_nextgen/`, outside Git.

The new manifest executor uses the existing CFBD client and budget counter but stores one ledger record per legal partition. It enforces three total attempts with backoff, writes atomic compressed Parquet, records schema/row/hash/status, and skips verified cache hits. Empty 200 responses and HTTP 400/401/403/404 enter `needs_review` and block stage progress; they do not establish permanent structural absence. A response at the documented cap is marked suspected partial and stops a stage; it needs documented subdivision before proceeding. The legacy `fetch_stats_basic_game` still catches per-week errors, so the nextgen config blocks that direct loop. Refresh 2026 only into quarantine. Predictive canonicalization uses completed regular games and excludes postseason numeric history even when raw legacy caches contain it.

The experiment root has `raw_cache`, `canonical`, `feature_families`, `fingerprints`, `experiments`, `results`, `feature_diagnostics`, `shap_tmp`, and `scratch`. The group filesystem reported about **1.09 TB available and 96% use** at the 2026-09-25 preflight; the **experiment soft budget is 100 GB**. One 2025 week-1 `/plays` sample compressed 16,693 rows into 648,159 bytes. The 2026-09-26 pre-Stage-A provisional total raw/canonical/intermediate estimate is **9.22 GB** (9,224,915,712 bytes), based on that play sample, the 95th percentile of legacy Parquet sizes, and conservative multipliers. This is a planning estimate, not measured full-run storage. Use a canonical superset plus lightweight manifests and clean temporary payloads, SHAP matrices, and routine logs.
