# Project History and Directions

This page reconstructs TDNet’s documented path into the 2026 confirmatory study and weekly scientific publication, while marking what the available records cannot establish.

## Evidence limits

The checked-out Git history begins with a public-release commit dated 2026-08-13, so it does not capture the project’s full origin or all pre-release decisions. Earlier research and planning are recoverable from dated documents, especially the long-running [TDNet Master Plan Source](Source-TDNet-Master-Plan). The sequence below is therefore a documented history, not a complete account of every project idea.

## How the research direction narrowed

The project moved from a broad fingerprint and model search toward a controlled study and frozen operation. F0–F8 remain visible in the scientific comparison; F0–F6 are market-free, while F7/F8 are market-bearing comparators. For weekly wide-margin operation, corrected full F6 was selected using margin MAE; F6-C compression was retained as supplemental analysis. A separate opponent-adjustment comparison selected v1.4 Elo context among allowed non-averaged methods, while v1.7 remained a comparator. Details and exact metrics live in [Fingerprint and Model Decision History](Fingerprint-and-Model-Decision-History).

The freeze also changed what “next work” meant: outcomes and scores from the prospective season were to be collected without altering the frozen 2026 system. New architectures, feature families, refits, or selection using 2026 outcomes belong to experimental or future-season work, not retrospective edits to the confirmatory roster. See [Deferred and Abandoned Directions](Deferred-and-Abandoned-Directions) and [Confirmatory Protocol 2026](Confirmatory-Protocol-2026).
